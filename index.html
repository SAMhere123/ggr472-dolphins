Our objective is to showcase the high diversity of dolphin species surrounding Hawaii, while also being aware of the effect tourism and climate change has on its population.
To use the map, select a species from the dropdown menu to view it individually, zoom in/out or return to the original extent, and click on the point locations to see more information about the species type, number observed, and date of the sighting.
